# 喵密尔顿 Miaomilton

一张会开演的 10 美元。A ten-dollar bill that stages *Hamilton*.

**▶ https://miaomiaomeowmeow.github.io/miaomilton/**

Part of **喵剧一堂 Miaosical** — a street of musicals, each entered through one iconic object. For *Hamilton*, the object is the $10 bill.

## 怎么玩 · How to play

- **点头像**：平面的人站起来，钞票上立起一座立体书小舞台；他离开的地方写出宪法开头「We the People」。
  Click the portrait: the engraved man stands up, a pop-up stage unfolds, and "We the People" writes itself into the space he left.
- **转转台**看三幕（顺时针下一幕，第三幕逆时针倒带）；点汉密尔顿或空白处收起舞台。
  Turn the stage for the three acts (clockwise = next; turn back to rewind *Satisfied*). Click Hamilton or empty space to fold it away.
- **找彩蛋**：火炬、红字、右下角会变色的 10，翻到背面还有水印和大楼。点左下角翘起的纸角翻面。
  Hidden scenes: the torch, the red script, the color-shifting 10 (tilt the bill), and on the back the watermark and the building. Click the curled corner to flip.
- 全部找到后，钞票正中出现一颗金星——点它，钞票折成星飞上夜空。
  Find everything and a gold star appears; click it and the bill folds into a star.
